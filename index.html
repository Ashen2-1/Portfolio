Mapping Greig's Caves with a handheld LiDAR–IMU rig

On September 4, 2026 I took my portable sensing platform (Livox Mid-360S, its built-in IMU, a Raspberry Pi camera, and a Pi-based button logger) into the limestone caves of the Niagara Escarpment. I recorded 11 sessions there, plus a follow-up session on Sep 7. Then I wrote a packet decoder and a LiDAR–inertial odometry pipeline to turn the raw logs into the 3D maps below.

01

A redundant logger saved the dataset

My logger wrote three streams at once: the Livox SDK2 recorder, a raw tcpdump capture of every LiDAR/IMU UDP packet, and H.264 video at 1536×864, 30 fps. In 6 of 12 sessions the SDK lost contact with the sensor ("Detection lidars failed") and its files stopped early. The raw pcap kept recording, and a steady ~200k pts/s across the whole capture shows those streams are complete. I wrote a pcap decoder for the Livox packet format and rebuilt every session from it.

02

"Static" scans weren't static, so motion had to be modeled

The IMU shows that even the "static" sessions were handheld sweeps, averaging 24–53 °/s of rotation. Stacking those points naively smears the cave walls. So the pipeline integrates the 200 Hz gyro to undo the rotation inside each 100 ms frame (deskew) and uses it as the rotation prior for registration.

03

LiDAR–inertial odometry, built from scratch

Each deskewed frame is aligned to a rolling 25 cm voxel map with robust point-to-point ICP (Gauss–Newton with a Geman–McClure kernel and an adaptive threshold, in the style of KISS-ICP), using a constant-velocity prior for translation. I wrote it in Python with NumPy and SciPy and ran it offline on all 12 sessions.

Pipeline

  1. CaptureMid-360S → Ethernet → Pi · SDK2 + raw pcap + camera
  2. DecodeCustom parser for Livox UDP: 96 pts/packet, IMU at 200 Hz
  3. DeskewGyro integration (SO(3)), per-point rotation to frame start
  4. RegisterRobust ICP to a local voxel map, IMU + constant-velocity priors
  5. MapGravity-aligned clouds, height maps, trajectories

Validated

Field logging with redundant capture · complete packet-level decode · IMU motion analysis · IMU-aided deskew · frame-to-map odometry producing coherent 3D maps of the cave sections.

Next
